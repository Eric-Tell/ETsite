The Gold Standard of Competitive Gaming

As one of the oldest and most established esports titles in history, League of Legends has defined modern competitive gaming. Run by Riot Games, regional leagues such as the LCK (Korea), LPL (China), LEC (EMEA), and LTA/Americas feed into the crown jewel of international competition: the annual World Championship ("Worlds").

With massive stadium turnouts, legendary players like Faker, and high-budget musical opening ceremonies, Worlds remains one of the most-watched annual sporting events on earth.
