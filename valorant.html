The Valorant Champions Tour (VCT)

Valorant, Riot Games' tactical first-person shooter, has taken the competitive gaming scene by storm since its launch. The official competitive ecosystem, known as the Valorant Champions Tour (VCT), features structured international leagues across the Americas, EMEA, Pacific, and China.

Teams battle through regional splits to qualify for prestigious global Masters tournaments and the year-end World Championship, known simply as Champions, where the best roster in the world is crowned.
